# Community conduct

In Foundation Math issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces:

- Treat people respectfully. Critique code and claims with specific evidence.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others, or publish private information.
- Avoid sexual content, personal attacks, repeated unwanted contact, and disruptive spam.
- Respect boundaries when someone asks you to stop contacting them.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive contributions, lock discussions, or
restrict participation according to the context and severity of the behavior.
This policy promises no response timeline or formal appeal process.

For a conduct concern, follow the private-channel guidance in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify the report as a conduct report. Do not post
personal or sensitive details publicly. GitHub's reporting and blocking tools
remain available for platform abuse.

This policy covers project-managed spaces, not unrelated communities.
